## Name
ScamTrapAlert

## Short description (132 chars max)
Detect scams, phishing & manipulation in WhatsApp, Gmail, LinkedIn & more with AI-powered scans.

## Detailed description
ScamTrapAlert helps you spot scams, phishing, and psychological manipulation before you reply or click.

**How it works**
- Open a chat, email, or job post on supported sites
- Click **Scan current tab** in the extension popup
- Get a risk score, scam type, highlighted dangerous sentences, and plain-English advice

**Supported sites**
- WhatsApp Web
- Gmail
- LinkedIn messages and job posts
- General web pages (manual scan)

**Privacy**
- No account required
- Text is sent to our secure server only when you scan
- See our privacy policy: [YOUR PRIVACY POLICY URL]

**Free tier**
- Daily scan limit (resets midnight UTC)

Upgrade plans may be offered in future updates.

## Category
Productivity (or Privacy & Security)

## Permission justification (dashboard fields)

**Read and change all your data on websites you visit**
Used only to read text on the page when you start a scan and to highlight risky sentences. We do not read pages in the background unless you enable auto-scan in Settings.

**Communicate with cooperating websites**
Required to connect to our analysis API over HTTPS.

## Single purpose declaration
Help users identify scam and manipulation risk in text on web pages they choose to analyze.
